Frequently asked questions

Is the app in English?

Not yet: the app is in Brazilian Portuguese, and it works with Brazilian banks and the real (R$). This page is in English so you can see what it does.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. You can enter everything by hand in seconds. If you want it automatic, the Family plan reads your bank's notifications on your phone and the Complete plan pulls statements through the bank integration, always with your permission.

Is it safe?

The connection is encrypted, the database has an encrypted daily backup and nobody on the team sees your amounts. You can download or delete your data whenever you want, as Brazil's data protection law (LGPD) requires.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days on the Family plan, no card needed. At the end you choose a plan. If you don't, nothing is deleted: you can still see and download everything, you just can't add new entries.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, right in the app, without talking to anyone. Your plan runs until the end of the paid period, and your data stays available to download.

Can the whole family use it?

Yes. The price is per family: everyone signs in with their own login, and the admin decides who sees each screen.

Does it work on my phone?

It works in your phone's browser, with shortcuts at the bottom to add, pay and see the forecast. You can add it to your home screen like an app.
